What changes, and why

  1. One type scale. 18 sizes → 7 (11/12/13/14/16/18/22), whole pixels only, each with a fixed role and paired line height. The 10.5px/11.5px/12.5px drift disappears. Timestamps and counters get tabular-nums so they stop wiggling.
  2. Fewer typefaces, used deliberately. Inter carries all UI; Outfit stays only for display moments (server name header, modal titles, login hero); JetBrains Mono only for code/numeric. DM Sans is retired — it’s a near-duplicate of Inter doing nothing distinctive — and the titlebar’s hardcoded Segoe UI override (which resolves differently on every OS) goes too.
  3. Weight discipline. Body 450 / UI labels 500 / emphasis 600. The variable fonts already support this for free. Usernames drop from 700 to 600 — they keep their per-user color, which does the identification work; today’s bold reads heavier than the chat body it sits on.
  4. One caps-label recipe. Today there are three (9px·700·wider, 10.5px·600·0.08em, 11px·500·0.12em). Proposed: 11px · 600 · 0.07em · text-muted everywhere — channel sections, members header, settings groups, form labels.
  5. Radius scale 6/10/14/20. The de-facto favorite rounded-[10px] (46 uses, off-scale) becomes the official mid step. Buttons, inputs, tiles and avatars share 10; rows/chips 6; cards/popovers/composer 14; modals 20. Avatars at radius 10 give Decibell a recognizable squircle identity vs Discord’s circles.
  6. Elevation tokens. 65 hand-rolled shadow-[…] values → raised / float / modal + one focus ring. Shadows also carry a faint 1px blue-tinted ring so raised surfaces separate cleanly without getting lighter.
  7. One surface ramp. 12 overlapping background tokens → 6 roles (chrome/sidebar/content/raised/high/input). Near-duplicates like #14151e vs #141820 merge. Slightly higher blue chroma overall; borders tint from pure white-alpha to blue-alpha for cohesion.
  8. Contrast fixes. secondary #7e8da6→#94a2ba, muted #4d5b72→#6b7890. Everything that renders at 11–12px (timestamps, section labels, status lines) climbs back above ~4.5:1.
  9. Accent unified, hue your call. #388fff vs #388bfd (slider, avatar palette) collapse into one token. Azure keeps today’s personality; Periwinkle moves further from GitHub; Teal is the most distinctive “audio brand” option. Status greens/ambers/reds warm up slightly to match.
  10. Motion tokens + a real bug. globals.css forces transition-duration: 75ms !important on every button/link, silently overriding each duration-200 written in components — your hover “lift” animations have never run at their authored speed. Proposed: 90ms for color/opacity, 150ms for transform, no !important; drop the hover translate jumps entirely (calmer, more “native” feel — the breathing live-glow stays).
